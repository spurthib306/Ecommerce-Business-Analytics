E-commerce Business Performance & Revenue

Olist Brazilian marketplace, orders purchased January 2017 to August 2018. Revenue is item price excluding freight, for orders that were not canceled or unavailable.

Built __BUILT__ from the cleaned dataset and validated against the SQL results.

Average order value = revenue / distinct orders. Late = delivered after the estimated date (delivered orders only). Review score is averaged per order.

Bar charts ignore their own filter and highlight the selection instead, like Power BI's highlight interaction. Every chart has a Table view.
